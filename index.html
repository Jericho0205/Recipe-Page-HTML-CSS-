Chicken Alfredo

A simple and creamy homade pasta recipe.

About This Recipe

This chicken alfredo combines pasta, chicken, parmesan cheese, and a creamy homemade sauce.

Learn more about Fettuccine Alfredo

Recipe Information

Prep Time: 15 minutes

Cook Time: 25 minutes

Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 12 oz fettuccine pasta
  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • 1 cup Parmesan cheese
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • Salt
  • Black pepper

Instructions

  1. Bring a large pot of water to a boil and cook the pasta.
  2. Season the chicken with salt and pepper.
  3. Cook the chicken in a pan until fully cooked.
  4. Remove the chicken and slice it into pieces.
  5. Add butter and garlic to the pan.
  6. Add the heavy cream and Parmesan cheese.
  7. Stir until the sauce becomes smooth and creamy.
  8. Add the pasta and chicken to the sauce.
  9. Serve and enjoy!
